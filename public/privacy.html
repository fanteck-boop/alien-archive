Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-23

Draft, not legal advice. Have someone qualified review this before it's relied on by real users.

Who this covers

Alien World ("the site", "we") is an unofficial, fan-made archive of the Alien media franchise. This policy covers what happens if you create an account to save favorites.

What we collect

  • Account data: if you sign in with email/password, we (via Firebase Authentication, a Google service) store your email address and an authentication credential. We never see or store your raw password.
  • Your favorites: the list of archive entries you've starred, stored in Firestore (also a Google service) under a record tied to your account.
  • Local-only data: if you don't sign in, favorites aren't saved anywhere and reset on reload.

We do not collect payment information, do not run advertising trackers, and do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing.

Why we collect it

Solely to let you keep your favorites list across visits and devices. That's the entire purpose of the account feature.

Third-party processors and data sources

Account and favorites data is processed by Google Firebase — see Google's Privacy Policy. Poster images and descriptions are fetched live from TMDB, RAWG, Comic Vine, and Open Library; browsing the archive sends the title you're viewing to these services via our server-side proxy, not directly from your browser.

Cookies

The site doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies. Firebase Authentication sets its own session storage to keep you signed in.

Your rights

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the "Delete my account and data" control next to Sign Out. This permanently removes your Firebase Authentication account and your Firestore favorites record. If you have trouble, contact us at fanteck.issues@gmail.com.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change. Continued use of the account feature after an update means you accept the revised policy.
